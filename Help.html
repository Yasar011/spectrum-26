Is Accommodation provided?

NO

Can I register on the spot?

Online registration is highly recommended to secure your Digital ID. Spot registration will be available but is subject to availability and higher fees.

What is the Refund Policy?

All ticket sales are final and non-refundable. However, tickets are transferable if the request is made 48 hours prior to the event via the Help Desk.

Are Pro-Shows included?

Yes, for Nexus Prime pass holders. Basic 'Cyber Entry' pass holders must purchase separate add-on tickets for the EDM Night and Fashion Show.
