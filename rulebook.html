General Terms & Conditions

  • During registration, all participants as well as visitors need to carry their any identity proof (Aadhar card, DL, Voter ID, etc.) and college identity cards.
  • Registration amount will not be refunded incase the participant fails to secure any position in the event.
  • If found involved in offensive activities, you will not be allowed to participate and with immediate effect thrown out of the college premises.
  • Participants need to adhere to the time limit of each event. Failure to do so will result in penalization per rules or disqualification.
  • Many events have themes/topics; every participant must stick to the topic or face a reduction in marks.
  • Judge's decision at the face of any discrepancy or dispute shall be final and binding. No further objections will be entertained.
  • Use of obscene, disrespectful, indecent, or offensive language, imagery, or implications are strictly prohibited.
  • Overstepping moral, social, or religious boundaries shall be immediately and strictly penalized.
  • The organizing committee reserves the right to change the event schedule and timings.
  • All participants should report to the venue at least 1 hour prior to the commencement of the event, unless mentioned.
  • Incase of any disruption, college management holds the right to change the list of winners.
  • If an event does not reach the minimum required registrations, the prize pool for that specific event will be decreased accordingly.
End of Directives.
