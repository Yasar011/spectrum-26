The Spirit of VIBHRAM

Vibhram is the art of distortion, the beautiful glitch in reality. As we explore the theme of "Synesthesia" for the Vogue Gala, we invite you to visualize sound and hear colors. Delve deeper into the core philosophy of our fest.

Event Timeline

Round 1: Conceptualization

Review Meeting 1

Participants must present to the jury panel:

  • Fashion illustrations of all 7 garments.
  • Theme, Mood, and Colour Boards aligning with "Synesthesia".

Presentation Time: 5 Minutes. Disqualification if concept does not align with theme.

Round 2: Prototype & Walk

Review Meeting 2

Teams required to present:

  • Two (2) finished garments/accessories on models.
  • Ramp walk choreography demonstration.
  • Downloaded music and video files for the show.

Round 3: Final Showdown

Final Review & Show Day

Final presentation requirements:

  • All 7 garments/accessories completed.
  • Final video and audio with changes suggested by faculty.

Teams failing to meet manufacturing or choreography standards will be eliminated.

Evaluation Parameters

  1. Concept & Theme: Depth of interpretation of "Synesthesia".
  2. Aesthetics: Garment overall look, construction quality, and styling.
  3. Performance: Choreography, music sync, and stage presentation.

Team Composition

At least 2 Designers (Max 3) + 1 Stylist.
Range: 7 Garments/Accessories.

General Rules

  • 01. Audio/Video files submission deadline: 26/02/25.
  • 02. Collection can be 100% self-stitched or up to 50% outsourced.
  • 03. Copyright infringement leads to immediate disqualification.
  • 04. Inappropriate content (songs/choreography) results in disqualification.
  • 05. Reporting time: 1 Hour prior to event at backstage.
  • 06. Construction quality must meet required standards.
