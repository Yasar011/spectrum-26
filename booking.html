Booking Procedure

01. Initialize Identity

Click the Login button on the top right. Create a new account using your email.
Note: NIFT Students must use their official @nift.ac.in email for free access.

02. Select Clearance Level

Navigate to Buy Tickets. Choose your Base Pass:

  • > Cyber Entry (₹299): Basic Entry Only.
  • > Nexus Prime (₹499): Entry + 2 Free Events + Priority Access.

03. Add Event Modules

Select the competitions or workshops you wish to participate in. If you have Nexus Prime, the first two regular events will automatically be marked as FREE in your cart.

04. Execute Transaction

Proceed to checkout. A UPI QR code will be generated.
1. Scan and Pay the total amount.
2. Take a Screenshot of the successful payment.
3. Upload the screenshot and enter the 12-digit UTR number.

05. Verification Protocol

Your pass will show as "Pending" in your Profile. Our team will verify the UTR and Screenshot within 2-4 hours.
Once verified, your Digital ID Card with a unique QR code will be activated.
