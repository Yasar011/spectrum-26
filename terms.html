Directives & Protocols

SPECTRUM '26 // VIBHRAM EDITION

01. ZERO REFUND POLICY

All ticket sales, including General Passes, Gold Passes, Add-ons, and Merchandise, are FINAL and NON-REFUNDABLE. No requests for refunds will be entertained under any circumstances, including but not limited to:

  • Personal scheduling conflicts.
  • Failure to carry valid ID cards.
  • Disqualification due to misconduct.

02. ENTRY & IDENTIFICATION

1. Mandatory ID: A valid, physical College or School ID Card is required for entry. Digital copies (DigiLocker/Photos) may be subject to stricter scrutiny or rejection.

2. General Pass Restriction: Holders of the General Pass (₹349) must exit the campus premises by 6:30 PM. Scanning systems will flag overstays.

3. Gold Pass Privilege: Gold Pass holders have unrestricted access to the venue for the duration of the event days.

03. EVENT RULES

  • EDM Night: Entry is strictly restricted to Gold Pass holders who have purchased the specific EDM Add-on.
  • Flagship Events: General Pass holders do not have audience access to Flagship events (Fashion Show, Group Dance, Battle of Bands, etc.) unless a specific add-on is purchased.
  • Code of Conduct: Any form of harassment, violence, or possession of illegal substances will result in immediate expulsion and handing over to authorities.
  • Prize Pools: If an event fails to meet the minimum required registrations, the prize pool for that specific event will be decreased accordingly.

04. LIABILITY

NIFT Jodhpur and the Spectrum Organizing Committee are not responsible for any loss, theft, or damage to personal belongings. Attendees enter the venue at their own risk.
